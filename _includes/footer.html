About this website

Paw Protection Alliance is a developing independent animal-protection initiative with a national focus. It is not incorporated, is not a registered nonprofit, and does not hold charitable tax-exempt status. "Paw Protection Alliance" is a working name.

PPA is an informational initiative, not an emergency animal rescue service.
