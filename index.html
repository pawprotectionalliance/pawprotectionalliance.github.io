Our Commitment

Protection Through Knowledge. Progress Through Action.

Paw Protection Alliance represents all animals, from wildlife and marine life to the companions who share our homes. Our leading priority is the animals raised for food, whose lives are shaped by systems most people never see.

Our mission is national, with farmed-animal welfare as a leading priority. Our first state-level legislation research begins in Missouri.

  • 01Credible researchSourced reporting on welfare, agricultural practices, and protection initiatives.
  • 02Public educationClear, accessible explanations of what animals need and what affects their lives.
  • 03Legislative awarenessPlain-language tracking of selected federal and state bills, beginning with Missouri.
  • 04Documented progressCareful source verification and clear documentation of our findings.
