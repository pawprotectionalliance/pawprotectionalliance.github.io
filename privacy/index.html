Last updated: November 13, 2026

Paw Protection Alliance (PPA) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through our website is handled.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, inquiry category, and message. Please do not include sensitive personal or financial information.

How We Use Your Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, review suggestions, address corrections, and manage correspondence. We do not sell contact information or use form submissions for marketing without separate consent.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is provided by Tally, which processes and stores form submissions. Notifications may also be delivered by email. Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. These services handle information according to their respective privacy policies.

Information Retention

We retain correspondence as needed to respond to inquiries and maintain appropriate records. You may request correction or deletion of information you have submitted.

External Links

Our website may link to independent organizations and external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

Questions or Privacy Requests

For questions about this policy or to request correction or deletion of submitted information, please use our Contact page.
