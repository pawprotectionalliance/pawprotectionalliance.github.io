How to read this tracker

Each record reflects the legislative session and the most recent action found in the cited official record, along with the date it was last checked. An older session may have ended even if its last recorded action says “In committee.” Summaries are written by PPA in plain language. Always rely on the official record for the bill's text and current status.

Facts and commentary are kept apart. The official record, status, and summary are factual. Where PPA offers a view, it appears in a separate box labeled "PPA commentary."
